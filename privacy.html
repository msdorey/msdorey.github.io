Privacy policy

local-assistant is used only by its owner. It asks Google for access to the owner's calendar events so the owner's assistant can read them and, with the owner's approval of each change, add or change them.

Calendar data is read and written on the owner's own PC. It is not stored on any other server, and it is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
